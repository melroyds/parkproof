# ParkProof product research, October 2026

Three research passes run before porting ParkProof to native iOS and Android, to
decide what the product should become as a consumer app.

**Read [00-synthesis.md](00-synthesis.md) first.** It is the readable version: what
was found, what changed, and the plan. The other three files are the full record.

| File | What it is |
|---|---|
| [00-synthesis.md](00-synthesis.md) | The findings, the overturned assumptions, the plan, pricing and the port decision |
| [01-market-landscape.md](01-market-landscape.md) | Competitors, Australian user pain, fines and appeals, family sharing, pricing benchmarks, emerging needs |
| [02-novel-use-cases.md](02-novel-use-cases.md) | Seven territories of use cases beyond "me and the sign", ranked and pruned |
| [03-workday-shuffle.md](03-workday-shuffle.md) | Whether and how to help commuters who move the car every two hours, including the road-rule legality |

## How it was done

Each pass ran several AI research agents in parallel, one per area, each required to
give a source URL for every claim or to say plainly that nothing was found. A separate
adversarial reviewer then re-fetched the load-bearing sources, struck unsupported
claims, merged duplicates and ranked what survived. Roughly 286 distinct sources across
the three passes.

## Reading the record

- Files 01 to 03 are reproduced **verbatim** from the agents' output. Nothing is
  paraphrased, so every source URL is preserved exactly.
- In each file, the **review section comes first and overrides** the individual reports
  where they disagree. Reports are numbered so the review's cross-references
  ("Report 3", "Territory 5") still resolve.
- Confidence ratings are the agents' own.
- Some sources failed review. The synthesis lists the ones that must not be cited.

Nothing here is legal advice. The road-rule findings were read from authorised
Victorian and Queensland legislation, but any in-app wording should be checked by
someone qualified before it ships.
