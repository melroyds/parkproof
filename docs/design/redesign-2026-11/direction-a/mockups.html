Plain Sign

ParkProof turns a chaotic pole into one plain-English sign. So the app speaks the kerb's own visual language: every answer is a sign plate, every record is a signed pass, and colour only ever means go, condition, stop or signed.

Colour is a road signal

A neutral ink-on-kerb base. Sign green means go, sign yellow means a condition or a doubt, sign red means stop, pine and mint mean signed. Nothing else gets colour, not even the brand.

Rounded is everyday, cut is signed

Everyday plates have sign-plate corners. Only signed artefacts are square-cut with the chamfer, on the dark vault. Seeing the vault means "ParkProof signed this".

1Home, currently parked

One skeleton. The parked plate stays white in every state; urgency rides on a band, the countdown colour and the bar. Check a sign is always the shutter in the same thumb spot.

2Result answer

Four designed states, not a boolean. The state is the plate's structure: top rule, tinted head and a shape-coded glyph. The commit is pinned and always says what it does.

3Parking record

State decides the layout. Active leads with time and actions; ended leads with the signed pass.

4Start parking

Logger and reminders merged into one docked sheet, then one crafted moment.

A grouped list on one surface, sorted by arrival.
